## What we collect

- **Account data**: email and a username/display name, stored in Supabase Auth.
- **Photos**: images you choose to upload as listings, stored in Supabase Storage (private bucket — only group members can view).
- **Auction data**: the groups you join, items you list, and bids you place.
- **Push tokens**: an Expo push token tied to your device, used only to notify you of bids and outcomes. Removed when you sign out or delete your account.
- **Crash and diagnostic data**: limited telemetry from Expo / EAS for debugging crashes. No third-party advertising SDKs.

## What we do NOT collect

- Location.
- Contacts, calendar, microphone, or audio.
- Health, financial, or biometric data.
- Browsing history outside the app.

## Who can see your data

- Only members of a group can see that group's listings, photos, bids, and member usernames.
- Reports of inappropriate content are visible to the app operators (us) and used to enforce community guidelines.
- We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

## How long we keep it

Account data and listings persist until you delete your account in **Profile → Delete account**, which removes everything (account, photos, listings, bids, tokens) immediately.

## Children

Closet Auction is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
